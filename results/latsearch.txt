the wall is the point of this table. d <= 8: the record is a root lattice and the
climb sits on it. From d = 9 the records are Lambda_9..12 (272, 336, 438, 648),
Barnes-Wall (4320 at d = 16) and the Leech lattice (196560 at d = 24) -- gluings of a
code with a lattice, not perturbations of a root lattice -- so a local search over Gram
entries cannot reach them. The shortfall is 1.26x at d = 9 and 178x at d = 24.

what this does not say: it is not a new bound in either direction. It says that 'run a
search in each dimension' is not a route to a better lower bound above d = 8: the lower
bounds there are code-theoretic (Leech, Barnes-Wall, P_9a..P_12a) and matching them
needs the codes, not a search.
