3. the corona, which is what is proved
   f0 = 607, f1 = 3828, kappa = 12.61285 = 7656/607
   the chain never reaches it: kappa_chain(10^6) = 12.5660 < 12.6129, and the limit is 666/53 = 12.5660

4. what the corona has that the chain does not
   chain increment per atom: (106, 666, 1120, 560); 107/667, so 607 vertices
   would need m = (607-14)/106 = 5.59 atoms -- not an integer, hence the corona is
   not a chain: it glues more facets per atom (each icosahedral facet meets another
   cell or a cap), and that extra gluing is the whole 12.566 -> 12.613 gain.

   to reproduce it one needs (i) the reflection group generated by the icosahedral
   facets of the cut 600-cell, (ii) the orbit of the cut cell under it, (iii) the
   set of cells meeting the cut cell (the corona), (iv) the caps that restore
   convexity at the icosahedral-tetrahedral ridges.  Items (i)-(iv) are finite
   combinatorial data; none of them is in this repository.
