  reading: on the torus (periodic family, no boundary) fcc has average degree
  exactly 12, and filling every octahedral hole also gives exactly 12: the
  interstitial trick is neutral, which is why the 7656/607 record needs a
  different construction.

  tetrahedral holes, by the same count: fcc has two per ball, each such ball has
  exactly 4 contacts, so filling them gives E = 6n + 8n = 14n on N = 3n balls,
  average 28/3 = 9.333 < 12 -- strictly worse than leaving the holes empty.
